# Slope and intercept

An affine line is a rule with two settings: a starting height and a constant change. This lesson connects those settings to points, then to a drawing. Check that you can calculate a signed difference and test a point in an equation ([L28](../L28/)).

## Objectives and prerequisites

- Identify slope.
- Identify the vertical intercept.
- Evaluate an affine function.
- Connect slope and change.
- Compare lines.
- Recognise limitations of slope-intercept form.

Prerequisites: L28. Domains and restrictions stated below are part of every rule.

## 1. Understand — A fixed and a variable price

**PDF situation, p. 38.** A mobile plan costs €10 per month plus €2 per GB used. Even if you use nothing, you pay €10. Each extra GB adds €2, not €10.

| Data used (GB) | Price calculation (€) | Total (€) |
| --- | --- | --- |
| 0 | $2\cdot0+10$ | 10 |
| 1 | $2\cdot1+10$ | 12 |
| 3 | $2\cdot3+10$ | 16 |
| 5 | $2\cdot5+10$ | 20 |

If x denotes usage and y price, the rule is $y=2x+10$. Usage is nonnegative in this model. The mathematical formula itself can be evaluated over all real numbers.

In the general form $y=mx+p$, **m is the slope (or gradient)**: the change in y per additional unit of x. **p is the vertical intercept**: the value of y when x is zero. Here m is €2/GB and p is €10. Their units differ!

A function $x\mapsto mx+p$ is affine. In the strict French mathematical sense, a linear function is the special case $p=0$.

## 3. Calculate — One operation at a time

### Read m and p, then calculate a value

For $y=-2x+5$, slope is −2 and the vertical intercept is 5. At x = 3:

$$y=-2\cdot3+5=-6+5=-1.$$

A negative slope means y decreases as x increases; it does not mean every vertical coordinate is negative. Here the line passes through $(0,5)$.

### Where does slope come from?

The Greek letter $\Delta$ (delta) means “change”: final value minus initial value. Subscripts 1 and 2 identify the two points, not powers.

Take $A=(0,1)$ and $B=(2,5)$ on the PDF line.

| Step | Calculation | Why? |
| --- | --- | --- |
| Horizontal | $\Delta x=2-0=2$ | Change in x |
| Vertical | $\Delta y=5-1=4$ | Change in y |
| Ratio | $m=4/2=2$ | Vertical change per horizontal unit |

**Why does the constant disappear?** For two points on the same line, $y_1=mx_1+p$ and $y_2=mx_2+p$. Subtract:

$$\Delta y=(mx_2+p)-(mx_1+p)=m(x_2-x_1)=m\Delta x.$$

The p term cancels. We get $\Delta y=m\Delta x$, then $m=\Delta y/\Delta x$ if $\Delta x\ne0$.

In the mobile plan, 4 extra GB cost €8 more, regardless of initial usage. For $y=-2x+5$, four additional units give $\Delta y=-2\cdot4=-8$.

### Plot a line without calculating many points

Example: $y=-\frac12x+2$.

1. Plot the vertical intercept: $(0,2)$.
2. Choose a horizontal displacement of 2 units: $\Delta x=2$.
3. Calculate the vertical step: $\Delta y=(-1/2)\cdot2=-1$.
4. The second point is $(2,1)$: move down by 1 from 2.
5. Check in the equation: $1=(-1/2)\cdot2+2$. Draw the line through both points.

For $y=\frac12x+1$, moving right by 2 and up by 1 from $(0,1)$ gives $(2,2)$. A fractional slope is a ratio, not a requirement to take a horizontal step of 1.

### Recognise boundary cases

| Case | Example | Interpretation |
| --- | --- | --- |
| Positive slope | $y=2x+1$ | Rises to the right |
| Negative slope | $y=-x+3$ | Falls to the right |
| Zero slope | $y=3$ | Horizontal, y does not change |
| Undefined slope | $x=2$ | Vertical, x does not change |

Two distinct points on a vertical line have zero horizontal difference: division by zero is impossible. A vertical line is not of form $y=mx+p$. On an affine line, choosing two coincident points also gives an undefined quotient, but the line's slope parameter still exists.

For equation $ax+by=c$, with $(a,b)\ne(0,0)$, if $b\ne0$, isolate y:

$$y=-\frac ab x+\frac cb.$$

Thus $m=-a/b$ and $p=c/b$. If b is zero, we obtain a vertical line.

Two lines with the same m but different p are distinct and parallel. Same m and same p: they coincide. For example $y=3x-1$ and $y=3x+4$ always have a vertical separation of 5 units (not necessarily their perpendicular distance).

**Another model:** $C(q)=3q+8$ represents a cost in euros for q items. Fixed cost is €8, slope is €3/item and $C(4)=3\cdot4+8=20$. Here q is a nonnegative integer.

## 5. Practise — Read, plot, explain

**Easy.** In $y=-3x+4$, find m, p and the vertical-axis crossing. Calculate the change in y when x increases by 2.

<details>
<summary>Check the answer</summary>

m is −3, p is 4 and the point is $(0,4)$. The change is $\Delta y=-3\cdot2=-6$. Move down six units; this is not the new vertical coordinate unless the starting coordinate is zero.

</details>

**Intermediate.** Plot $y=\frac34x-2$ from its vertical intercept and an integer step.

<details>
<summary>Check the answer</summary>

Start at $(0,-2)$. Take $\Delta x=4$ and calculate $\Delta y=(3/4)\cdot4=3$. The second point is $(4,1)$. Check: $1=(3/4)\cdot4-2$.

</details>

**Challenge.** Do equations $2x+4y=8$ and $y=-\frac12x+1$ describe distinct parallel lines? Does a vertical line have zero slope?

<details>
<summary>Show the worked solution</summary>

Isolate y in the first equation: $4y=-2x+8$, hence $y=-\frac12x+2$. The slopes are equal and the vertical intercepts differ: the lines are distinct and parallel. A vertical line has undefined slope; zero slope gives a horizontal line.

</details>

## 6. Remember — Printable revision sheet

- Form of a nonvertical line: $y=mx+p$; start $(0,p)$.
- Slope or gradient: $m=\Delta y/\Delta x$ if $\Delta x\ne0$; change: $\Delta y=m\Delta x$.
- Plot: place the start, choose a nonzero horizontal step, calculate the vertical step, check the second point.
- Positive m: rises; negative m: falls; zero m: horizontal. Vertical: undefined slope.
- Same m, different p: distinct parallel lines. Same m and p: same line.
- Traps: confusing coordinate and change, reversing the ratio, forgetting the sign, dividing by zero.
