## Third-party course source

The planning source is Bruno Teheux's *Mathématiques Générales - Logique et ensembles*, BINFO-FCE 2026-2027, University of Luxembourg. Source references use PDF page numbers, starting with the title page as page 1.

The source PDF, slide artwork, and copied third-party course material are not included in this repository and are not covered by this project's MIT licence. Publication/adaptation permission has not been verified. Attribution alone is not a record of permission.

Chapter 2 also references Bruno Teheux's *Mathématiques Générales — Droites: du papier à l'écran, puis au machine learning*, an 87-page supplied PDF. The source-aligned readings use original explanations, mathematical examples and newly plotted teaching SVGs; they do not redistribute the PDF or its artwork. Historical numerical observations are attributed in the reading. The classical Anscombe numeric dataset is identified separately with links to the R dataset source and documentation; the SVG compositions are original project work.

Author original explanations and exercises, identify their authorship, and cite the course pages that inform coverage. Do not add source PDFs, screenshots, or substantial copied course text without a recorded rights decision. An independent project must not claim university endorsement.
