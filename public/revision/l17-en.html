L17 — Relations and quantifier order

Method

∀x ∃c: at least one true cell in every row. ∃c ∀x: an all-true column. ∃x ∀c: an all-true row. The product includes both true and false cells.

Written practice

1. For E = {a,b} and C = {p,q}, enumerate the product and choose a relation where everyone has a course but no course is shared.

2. On the PDF data, give a witness for one student taking every course and a counterexample to every student taking at least one course.

3. Explain why one course shared by all implies everyone has a course, but not conversely.
