L11 — Predicates and quantifiers

1. Domain, predicate and evidence

∀: for every. ∃: there is at least one. Fix X, then evaluate P(x).

StatementTrueFalse
∀ x ∈ X, P(x)All satisfy POne counterexample
∃ x ∈ X, P(x)One witnessNo witness

Empty domain: ∀ vacuously true, ∃ false. An object outside X is never a witness or counterexample. On an infinite domain a few trials are not a general proof.

2. Your turn

X = {0, 2, 4}, P(x): x > 3. Give a witness and counterexample; justify both statements.

On ℝ, justify ∀ x (x² ≥ 0) and ∃ x (x² = −1).
