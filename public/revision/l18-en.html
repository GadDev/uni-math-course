L18 — Queries, predicates and counterexamples

Method

WHERE filters rows; EXISTS means at least one row; NOT EXISTS means no rows. Express ∀ by looking for the absence of a counterexample: NOT EXISTS of a failure. A correlated subquery depends on the outer row.

Written practice

1. On the PDF data, what does SELECT i.etudiant FROM Inscrit i WHERE i.cours = 'prog' return?

2. Write a query returning students with no enrolment, then explain correlation.

3. After adding (cleo, math), is every student enrolled in a course? Justify using NOT EXISTS.
