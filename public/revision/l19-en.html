L19 — Indices, comparisons and short-circuiting

Method

Sorted: compare adjacent i and i+1, with i in K. Duplicate: find i < j with equal values. Maximum: find an index i dominating every j. all on empty = True; any on empty = False.

Written practice

1. For t = [1,3,2], give the valid start indices and the comparisons executed by all.

2. For t = [2,2,5,5], give a duplicate witness pair and all maximum indices.

3. For t = [], give the truth values of sorted, not sorted, has a duplicate and contains its maximum.
