## Review decisions

For E01, the literal French reading has one valid option (`10`), while the literal English reading has three (`00`, `01`, `11`). Both cannot share the current single-answer key. Treating the change as cosmetic is rejected because every English option's grade reverses its mathematical validity. Changing only the shared key is rejected because it would break French and still omit two English answers. Aligning the English false-case stem in a reviewed new version preserves the objective and the verified key. Severity is blocking because meaning and grading change; a minor wording classification would understate the effect.
