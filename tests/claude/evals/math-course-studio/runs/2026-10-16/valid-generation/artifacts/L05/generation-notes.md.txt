## Editorial difficulty

All items require L01 truth values and L02 AND, inclusive OR and NOT; L05 definitions are taught before this bank. Arithmetic is deliberately small. Natural numbers include zero; no later L10 number-set theory or L06 contrapositive theorem is required. Optional hints cue a method rather than naming an option. Returning learners may need a short refresher on greater-than versus greater-than-or-equal before E06.

## Answer-key verification

| Item | Key | Definition-based author check | Different check and edge cases |
| --- | --- | --- | --- |
| E01 | 10 | The sole broken promise has true antecedent and false consequent. | Rewrite as NOT A OR B; bundled parser checks all four assignments. Both false and both true cases included. |
| E02 | 00 true; 01 true; 10 false; 11 true | Implication failure rule applied to four rows. | Parser evaluates NOT A OR B through implication semantics and agrees with all rows. |
| E03 | sufficient | Ordered numbers above 8 are above 3; 5 shows the converse fails. | Contradicting the forward direction would require n > 8 and n ≤ 3, impossible by order. Enumeration 0..12 additionally checks direction and witness; bounded enumeration alone is not an infinite-domain proof. |
| E04 | failure | True whole antecedent and false consequent require A AND B AND NOT C. | De Morgan rewrite of NOT(NOT(A AND B) OR C), plus parser comparison over all eight assignments, agrees. Parser also rejects all three distractor equivalences. |
| E05 | red-unstamped | Each selected card could conceal a red unstamped card; neither excluded card can violate the rule. | Exhaustive enumeration of 16 hidden-side models and all 16 inspection subsets yields unique smallest subset (red, unstamped). Each selected card can be the sole violation, establishing necessity; selecting all works but is not minimal. |
| E06 | n4 | At 4 the antecedent is true and consequent false. | Solve n ≥ 4 AND n ≤ 4, giving only 4 over all natural numbers; enumeration 0..12 agrees. Explicitly test zero, values below the boundary and 5 above it. |

The executable propositional checks are in `answer-checks.json`. The reproducible `verify-contexts.py` assertion check additionally enumerated the stated numeric range and card models; its assertions all passed. No proof grader is claimed.
