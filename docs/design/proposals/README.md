# Design direction and archive

The selected **Signal** identity is implemented with strong editorial typography, black rules, vermilion accents and original geometry. Public pages use semantic HTML, accessible mathematical notation and live controls, not the early raster mockups. For the actual implementation, screenshots and limits, see [public reading review](../implementation/signal-public-reading/README.md).

The early nine-screen Signal gallery, its proposed component rules, sample-content review and image-conversion evidence were design exploration, **not** production or accessibility acceptance. To keep the working repository lean, that historical concept gallery and its large WebPs were retired. They remain available unchanged at the [pre-cleanup revision](https://github.com/GadDev/uni-math-course/tree/abac4cb4df7aa3fab55c69a42f38018bb85364cc/docs/design/proposals), including [all nine preview images](https://github.com/GadDev/uni-math-course/tree/abac4cb4df7aa3fab55c69a42f38018bb85364cc/docs/design/proposals/images) and their evidence.

Chapter 2's separately selected **Décalage green** proposal, editable SVG, source studies and validation evidence remain in [the selected Chapter 2 pack](chapter-02-droites/selected/README.md). The actual [Chapter 2 implementation captures](../implementation/chapter-02/README.md) are also retained. They, rather than the obsolete Signal concept gallery, provide the useful current visual reference.

Artwork alone cannot establish mathematical correctness, translation fidelity, browser accessibility or hosted pilot readiness. Those checks belong to the implementation tests and [pilot acceptance](../../PILOT_ACCEPTANCE.md).
