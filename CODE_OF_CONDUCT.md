# Code of conduct

This learning community welcomes classmates and contributors with different backgrounds, languages, and levels of mathematical confidence.

## Expected behaviour

- Be respectful, patient, and constructive.
- Critique an argument or implementation rather than the person.
- Explain corrections clearly and make room for questions.
- Respect privacy and credit other people's work.

Harassment, discriminatory language, intimidation, personal attacks, and publishing private information without consent are unacceptable.

## Reporting and enforcement

The maintainer, [GadDev](https://github.com/GadDev), handles reports and enforcement. Use a private contact method listed on the maintainer's profile. If none is available, make a brief request for a private contact channel without including names, allegations, or sensitive details in a public issue.

Reports will be handled discreetly. The maintainer may request a correction, remove content, restrict participation, or ban a contributor depending on the behaviour and its impact. A report about the maintainer can also be escalated using GitHub's reporting channels.

This policy applies to repository discussions, issues, pull requests, and project community spaces.
